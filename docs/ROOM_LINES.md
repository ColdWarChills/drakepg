## Brindlemoor and the Thornwood

**Hunters' Camp** (quiet, campfire)
- Scene: A hunters' camp, left in a hurry. The fire pit is still warm.
- Scene: Far off in the trees, something old creaks, like a door that has not opened in a hundred years.
- Torn note: The boars are running the wrong way: out of the deep wood, not into it. / Whatever woke in the Hollow is not hunting them. It is pushing them out. We leave at first light. B.
- Scored log: Deep grooves across a fallen trunk. Not claws: roots, dragged through the wood like fingers.

**Woodcutters' Clearing** (quiet)
- Scene: A clearing full of fresh stumps. The axes are still stuck in them.
- Scene: The woodcutters left in a hurry. Deeper in, the trees grow so close the light turns green.
- Guild sign: Woodcutters' Guild. No cutting past this sign. By order of Elder Maren. / Carved underneath, fresh: THE TREES CUT BACK.
- Old stump: Cut a week ago, by the look of it. It already has new shoots as long as your arm.

**Bramble Hideout** (combat)
- Bandit's tally: Purses taken: 31. Lads lost to the trees: 6. / The forest is worse than the guards. Last night the brambles moved the path again.
- Stolen crates: Crates stamped with Tobin's mark. So that is where his spring stock went.

**Briar Wall** (quiet, campfire)
- Scene: The path ends in a wall of thorns as tall as a house.
- Scene: It creaks in and out, slow and even, like something asleep behind it is breathing.
- Old bones: A deer, caught in the thorns and never let go. The vines grew right through it.
- Thorns: The thorns lean toward you while you look. When you step back, they settle.

## Mirewater Fen

**Eel Pools** (combat)
- Warning post: Scratched into the post: DON'T HIT THE EELS WITH LIGHTNING. / Under it, in another hand: we know now.

**Stilt Village** (quiet, campfire)
- Scene: Fishers' huts on tall stilts, all empty. The boats are tied up high, out of the water's reach.
- Scene: Out past the reeds, something big rolls over in the mud, and the whole pool slops against the stilts.
- Fisher's slate: Toads in the nets again, twice the size of last year's. / Mother is feeding them. We leave for Reedwatch tomorrow.
- Gnawed stilt: A stilt as thick as a man, bitten halfway through. The tooth marks are the size of your hand.

**Shrine Steps** (quiet, campfire)
- Scene: Old stone steps lead down into the water. The rain falls sideways here.
- Scene: Ahead, lightning strikes the same drowned stone, again and again, as if something down there is calling it.
- Worn carving: A shrine to the river, older than Reedwatch. / Someone has scratched a coiled serpent over the old carvings, again and again.
- Offerings: Fish bones and copper coins on the top step. Fresh. Someone still comes here to ask the storm for mercy.

## Frostfang Mountains

**Hot Springs** (quiet, campfire)
- Scene: Warm water steams in a deep cave pool. For the first time since the pass, your fingers stop aching.
- Scene: Whatever is chilling the mountain, its spell stops at the water's edge.
- Old note: Gone for water. If you read this, bring a bucket. M. / The ink has run in the steam. Nobody from Hearthold has made it down here since the snows came.

**Ranger's Lookout** (quiet, campfire)
- Scene: A rangers' lookout, half buried in snow. The fire pit is cold and the door hangs open.
- Scene: Further on, at the mouth of a den, a great white shape paces back and forth. Back and forth.
- Ranger's log: Day 40. The old bear has not slept all winter. She paces the den mouth like a dog on a chain. / Day 41. A woman in white came down from the roost. She spoke to the bear, and the bear sat. Going closer tomorrow. T.
- Clawed door: Deep claw marks on the door, on the outside. Whatever made them wanted in, then gave up.

**Roost Stair** (quiet, campfire)
- Scene: Steps cut into the mountain, older than Hearthold. Blue runes glow in the ice on either side.
- Scene: At the top, a light flickers behind a wall of ice. The wind sings one long, cold note.
- Rune pillar: The same rune Sabine found in the golem's core. Up close, the ice hums. / This cold is not weather. Someone is making it.
- Footprints: Small bare footprints in the snow. Whoever made them does not feel the cold.
