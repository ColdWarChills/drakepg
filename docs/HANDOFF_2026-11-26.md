## 2. Where the game stands

The live branch is `claude/sweet-cray-w6r64t`. All of these are in the Unity game there, built 2026-11-25 and 26 by the roadmap session:

- **The defeat card says why you lost.** It shows the biggest hit, what resisted you, and the weak spots, with Try again at the boss door.
- **A boss's first kill gets its own card.** The boss is shown with a FIRST KILL stamp, along with the turns taken and what the win opens.
- **"New!" shows one card per new thing,** each with a picture.
- **There is an Options page.** Every key can be changed, and shake, flashes and volume can be set.
- **Paper damage is on every monster:**
  - Hits leave creases and throw red paper scraps.
  - Tears open at one third and at 15% health.
  - Fire burns holes.
  - Ice frosts the paper, and the next hit snaps a piece off.
  - Energy rips a tear.
  - Death is a crumple.
  - Water was dropped after two tries.
  - The cheat menu's "Paper monsters" switch turns all of it off.

The records are in `docs/IMPROVEMENTS_2026-10.md`.

## 7. How the designer wants the work done

The rules are in CLAUDE.md "Working with the designer". The ones most often broken:
- One job at a time. Say in one line what is being made and what the designer will see.
- Every step ends in something to look at.
- Judge it first, beside today's game, at 3x for figures. A "partly" is a no.
- Show only the new result.
- Two failed tries, then stop.
- Ask at most one or two questions, about something the designer has seen, in everyday words, with Claude's pick.
- Replies are 2-4 plain sentences.
- Never relay a session's "it reads well" unchecked.
- The weapon is in the hero's right (near) hand.
