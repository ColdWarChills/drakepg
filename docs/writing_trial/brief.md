# Writing brief: two pieces of game text

You are writing text for an original browser fantasy RPG (turn-based battles, single-screen rooms, a flat cut-paper art look). Players read at a glance, so every line must be short and plain.

## Rules for every line
- Plain everyday words, short sentences, concrete things the player can picture.
- A little dry humour is welcome. No purple prose, no modern slang, no fourth-wall jokes.
- Use only the names given below. Invent no new named people, places or monsters.
- Every fact listed under a piece must be there. Add nothing that changes the story.
- Keep to the line counts and word limits exactly.
- Reply with the output format at the end and nothing else.

## World facts
- Brindlemoor is a small town. East of it lies the Thornwood, a forest.
- Since "the old dragon went quiet", the Thornwood has grown restless: imps, boars, bandits and moss creatures trouble the roads.
- Elder Maren is Brindlemoor's town elder: warm, worried, a little formal. She gives the hero work.
- The hero is a new adventurer, spoken to as "you". Their class is unknown (warrior, mage or rogue), so never name a weapon type.

## Piece 1: the quest "Heart of Thorns" (Elder Maren speaks; the end of Chapter 1)

Offer: exactly 3 lines, each at most 30 words. Shown when she gives the quest. Facts:
- The moss, the bandits and the imps all point to one cause.
- An ancient heartwood tree has woken in the Hollow past the Deep Thornwood. It is called the Thornheart.
- The thorn wall that blocked the path to the Hollow will part for the hero now.
- A battle tip that must be clear: when the Thornheart gathers its strength for a big blow, the hero should Defend or heal. ("Defend" is a battle button; keep that word.)

Reminder: exactly 1 line, at most 16 words. Shown each time the player talks to her before the fight. Facts: where the Thornheart is, and buy potions first.

Complete: exactly 3 lines, each at most 30 words. Shown after the hero wins. Facts:
- The forest is already quieter.
- She gives the hero a weapon carved from the Thornheart itself.
- She fears this is only the beginning, but tonight Brindlemoor celebrates the hero.

## Piece 2: three things the player reads in the Thornwood

Each is 1 or 2 lines, each line at most 22 words: what the player sees or reads on the object.

a) "Torn note", found in an abandoned hunters' camp, written by a hunter who signs with one initial, B. Facts: the boars are running out of the deep wood, not into it; whatever woke in the Hollow is not hunting them, it is pushing them out; the hunters leave at first light.

b) "Guild sign" at the woodcutters' clearing. Facts: the official sign says no cutting past this point, by order of Elder Maren; underneath, someone has freshly carved a warning that the trees are fighting back.

c) "Old stump" in the same clearing. Facts: it was cut about a week ago, but it already has new shoots as long as an arm.
