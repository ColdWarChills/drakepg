# Writing brief: two pieces of game text, kept very short

You are writing text for an original browser fantasy RPG (turn-based battles, single-screen rooms, a flat cut-paper art look). Players read at a glance and skip anything long, so every line must be short and plain. Shorter is better: a line well under the limit beats one at the limit.

## Rules for every line
- At most 12 words per line. Count them.
- Plain everyday words, short sentences, concrete things the player can picture.
- A little dry humour is welcome if it costs no extra words. No purple prose, no modern slang, no fourth-wall jokes.
- Use only the names given below. Invent no new named people, places or monsters.
- Every "must" fact under a piece must be there. "Optional" facts only if there is room. Add nothing that changes the story.
- Keep to the line counts and word totals exactly.
- Reply with the output format at the end and nothing else.

## World facts
- Brindlemoor is a small town. East of it lies the Thornwood, a forest.
- Since "the old dragon went quiet", the Thornwood has grown restless: imps, boars, bandits and moss creatures trouble the roads.
- Elder Maren is Brindlemoor's town elder: warm, worried, a little formal. She gives the hero work.
- The hero is a new adventurer, spoken to as "you". Their class is unknown (warrior, mage or rogue), so never name a weapon type.

## Piece 1: the quest "Heart of Thorns" (Elder Maren speaks; the end of Chapter 1)

Offer: 2 or 3 lines, at most 30 words in total. Shown when she gives the quest.
- Must: an ancient heartwood tree, the Thornheart, has woken in the Hollow past the Deep Thornwood.
- Must: the thorn wall that blocked the path to the Hollow will part for the hero now.
- Must: a clear battle tip: when the Thornheart gathers its strength for a big blow, Defend or heal. ("Defend" is a battle button; keep that word.)
- Optional: the moss, the bandits and the imps all come from this one cause.

Reminder: exactly 1 line, at most 10 words. Shown each time the player talks to her before the fight.
- Must: where the Thornheart is. Optional: buy potions first.

Complete: 2 or 3 lines, at most 30 words in total. Shown after the hero wins.
- Must: the forest is already quieter.
- Must: she gives the hero a weapon carved from the Thornheart itself.
- Must: she fears this is only the beginning.
- Optional: tonight Brindlemoor celebrates the hero.

## Piece 2: three things the player reads in the Thornwood

Each is 1 or 2 lines, at most 20 words in total: what the player sees or reads on the object.

a) "Torn note", found in an abandoned hunters' camp, written by a hunter who signs with one initial, B. Must: the boars are running out of the deep wood, not into it; something in the Hollow is pushing them out; the hunters are leaving.

b) "Guild sign" at the woodcutters' clearing. Must: the sign says no cutting past this point, by order of Elder Maren; underneath, someone has freshly carved a warning that the trees are fighting back.

c) "Old stump" in the same clearing. Must: it was cut about a week ago, but it already has new shoots as long as an arm.

## Output format (exactly this, plain text; leave out a line you don't use)

OFFER 2: ...
OFFER 3: ...

COMPLETE 1: ...
COMPLETE 2: ...
COMPLETE 3: ...
NOTE: ... (one or two lines; separate two lines with " / ")
