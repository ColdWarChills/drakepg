## 6. Paper damage: what it is

The idea: every character is a paper cut-out, and damage happens to the paper. One system for any sprite, with no setup per monster.

What the browser mock does (`art/tests/paper_damage_mock.html`; its script is the reference for the behaviour, not for Unity code):

- Each hit leaves a crease: a line across the sprite at a random place and angle.
- Tears open by themselves at one third and at 15% health. Hits throw red paper scraps instead of blood.
- Elements act on the paper: fire burns holes; water soaks it and it sags; ice frosts it and the next hit snaps a piece off; energy rips a tear. Fire dries wet paper and melts frost to wet. Healing tapes the tears and patches the holes.
- At death the paper folds along the creases the fight left, so no two deaths match: a few creases give a few neat folds, many give a fast rough crumple. Then it drops and bounces.
- Intended for Unity: real physics only for the loose bits (scraps, snapped pieces, the final drop). The folding itself is driven by the creases, so it needs no setup per sprite.

What is not solved:

- **The crumple.** In the mock it still reads as fast folding into a shaded lump. The designer wants better than the mock.
- **Unity.** Nothing here has been tried in Unity. The browser chat does not know which Unity technique fits, and does not know whether it works on a bone-rigged sprite (the hero).

The proof (roadmap item, 1 week, needs his yes): one monster in Unity with creases, tears, red scraps, one elemental reaction and the death fold, beside the same monster as it is today. Stop rule: two failed tries and the idea is dropped.
