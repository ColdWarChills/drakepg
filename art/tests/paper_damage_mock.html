Paper damage mock

Your monsters as paper that takes the damage. One set of effects works on any picture: nothing here was drawn for these two. A rough browser sketch to judge the idea, not how it would look in Unity.

  • Hit: red paper scraps fly and stay on the floor, and each blow leaves a crease where it landed. Tears open at one-third health.
  • Death follows the fight. The paper folds along its own creases, so no two deaths match. Three heavy hits leave few creases and it folds up neatly. Nine small hits leave many and it crumples into a wad.
  • Fire burns holes with a glowing edge. Water soaks it dark and makes it sag. Ice frosts it stiff, and the next hit snaps a piece off. Energy rips a jagged tear.
  • They react to each other: fire on wet paper only steams it dry, and fire on frost melts it into wet paper.
  • Heal smooths the creases, tapes the tears and patches the holes.
  • The tick box is the other rule: the room goes a step darker and only the effects glow. Untick it to compare.
