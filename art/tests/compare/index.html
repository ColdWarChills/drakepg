Notes

LoRA: Soyuzmultfilm (Civitai, selling images OK)
Looks the most hand-made and the least AI; flat colours and clean outlines are the easiest to cut into rig parts and keep matching. Risk: simple, a bit retro. Rig fit: best.

LoRA: Disco Elysium (Civitai, selling images OK)
Richest mood, but this LoRA paints ground shadows and broke the boss's anatomy most often (needed the most re-rolls); swords come point-down (flipped here). Soft edges hide rig seams; its no-arms edit kept the sleeves, so only the forearms swing. Rig fit: good.

No LoRA: plain Qwen + a snap to a real pixel grid (4x, 64 colours)
Crisp and consistent with no LoRA at all; but rotating parts breaks the pixel grid (jaggy arm in the rig), so it suits frame-by-frame animation more than cut-out. Rig fit: weakest.

LoRA: Sidney Sime (Civitai, selling images OK)
Most storybook charm, steady linework across the set; the room is busy and it draws swords point-down (flipped here). Ink lines hide rig seams. Rig fit: good.
